# Skim dialogs, menus and snackbars

Every overlay and where it opens from (board 15). Dialogs are 344 wide on surfaceContainerHigh with radius 28. Menus use surfaceContainer with radius 16 and a gap between groups. Snackbars use inverseSurface and sit above the floating toolbar.

## Add shortcut

Home › Add (04). Edit shortcut in the shortcut menu opens the same dialog, filled in.

Text on the board: Add shortcut; Name; URL; Cancel; Save; Daylight Journal; daylightjournal.com

## Search engine

Settings › Search engine (12). Tapping an option saves it and closes the dialog.

Text on the board: Search engine; Google; Bing; DuckDuckGo; Ecosia; Cancel

## Theme

Settings › Theme (12). Applies at once. Dark uses the dark scheme in 17.

Text on the board: Theme; System default; Light; Dark; Cancel

## Clear browsing data

Settings (12) and History (13). Clear is disabled while nothing is checked.

Text on the board: Clear browsing data; Removes data saved on this phone, from all time.; Browsing history; Cookies and site data; Signs you out of most sites; Cached images and files; Skim history is managed in AI & privacy.; Cancel; Clear

## Delete all AI data

AI & privacy (10). Clears Skim history and every site's AI permission.

## Clear Skim history
