Why heat pumps are winning in cold climates

By Marta Lindqvist
auto_awesome Why heat pumps are winning in cold climates daylightjournal.com
auto_awesome Summary
  • Modern cold-climate heat pumps keep heating at minus 25 °C, so the old "not below freezing" rule no longer holds.
  • The gains come from variable-speed compressors and refrigerants that stay efficient in cold air.
  • Switching from oil saves roughly $300 to $900 a year; from gas the savings are smaller and depend on local prices.
How much would I save per year?

The article puts it at $300 to $900 a year for a home switching from oil heating. Switching from gas saves less, and the exact figure depends on local energy prices.
