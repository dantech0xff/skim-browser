Tokens

Material 3 Expressive tokens for Skim in Jetpack Compose. Sizes are dp and text sizes sp. Android 12 and later use dynamic color from the wallpaper;the brand fallback below is SchemeVibrant (spec 2025) from seed #1A56DB. Components, theme code and build rules are on 18.

At a glance

Motion, spacing and elevation
