Why heat pumps are winning in cold climates

Marta Lindqvist

For decades the advice was simple: if your winters drop well below freezing, a heat pump is not for you. That advice is now out of date, and the numbers coming out of Norway, Finland and the northern United States show how quickly it has aged.

Cold-climate models from several manufacturers keep delivering useful heat at minus 25 °C.
